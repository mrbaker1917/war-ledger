A side-by-side account of what the war has cost Ukraine and Russia. Compiled 16 November 2026. Not a live feed.

Independent estimates put Russian military deaths at about three times Ukrainian military deaths. Civilian deaths, civilian injuries, and wrecked infrastructure are documented at national scale in Ukraine. Russia has not been measured the same way, which is not the same thing as zero.

How to read a row

  1. Shared scale. Bars are drawn only when both numbers measure the same thing. An empty hatched track means no comparable national figure, not a count of zero.
  2. A range is an estimate. The solid part of a bar is the low end. The pale part runs up to the high end.
  3. Claims stay labeled. A government figure about its own losses, and a government figure about the other side, are never treated as one measurement.
